## Overview

This CloudOps engagement focused on creating a disaster recovery strategy that could recover essential services quickly and predictably after a system or region failure. The design prioritized automation, repeatability, and measured recovery time objectives.

---

## Key Highlights

- Provisioned multi-AZ and multi-region infrastructure with Terraform for consistent recovery paths.
- Automated failover workflows for networking, data services, and compute layers.
- Configured backups, replication, and dependency sequencing to meet recovery objectives.
- Built verification runbooks to validate backup integrity before production cutover.
- Aligned architecture and operational documentation with RTO/RPO requirements.


---

## Outcome

Critical workloads gained stronger resiliency and faster restoration capabilities, reducing business downtime risk during system disruptions.
