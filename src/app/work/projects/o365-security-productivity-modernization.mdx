## Overview

This Office 365 initiative focused on turning Microsoft 365 into a secure and efficient collaboration platform for employees across multiple locations. The design reduced risk while enabling productivity gains for everyday work.

---

## Key Highlights

- Configured Defender for M365, conditional access, and secure collaboration controls.
- Standardized Teams, SharePoint, and Exchange usage to improve consistency and adoption.
- Automated user lifecycle and access review processes for governance and compliance.
- Improved retention, security posture, and device compliance monitoring for remote workers.
- Created adoption dashboards and support materials for business teams and administrators.


---

## Outcome

The organization gained a more secure and productive Microsoft 365 environment that supported remote work while maintaining governance and user confidence.
