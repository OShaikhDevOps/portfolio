## Overview

This initiative modernized demand forecasting by combining machine learning engineering with platform automation. It created a repeatable workflow from data ingestion to production monitoring, ensuring the ML lifecycle remained reliable and auditable.

---

## Key Highlights

- Built ingestion and transformation pipelines for historical sales, inventory, and external demand signals.
- Automated feature engineering and model evaluation using scoring metrics tuned to forecast accuracy.
- Added drift and data quality alerts to detect changes in seasonality, demand spikes, and feature distribution.
- Enabled model promotion and rollback through environment-aware deployment rules and approval gates.
- Created operational dashboards for model health, prediction quality, and anomaly detection.


---

## Outcome

Forecast accuracy improved through consistent retraining and governance, while the business gained faster decision-making with lower manual operational overhead.
