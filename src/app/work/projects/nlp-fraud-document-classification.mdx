## Overview

This ML Engineering initiative used natural language processing to classify incoming documents and detect suspicious patterns with greater speed and consistency than traditional manual triage.

---

## Key Highlights

- Processed large text corpora from claims, invoices, applications, and support tickets.
- Built preprocessing and feature pipelines for embeddings, tokenization, and classification workflows.
- Trained and evaluated models for fraud signal detection and document type routing.
- Created explainability and confidence scoring to support operator review and governance.
- Deployed models behind APIs with batch scoring and monitoring for drift and confidence degradation.


---

## Outcome

The platform improved routing speed and reduced manual review burden while helping teams identify risk earlier and more consistently.
