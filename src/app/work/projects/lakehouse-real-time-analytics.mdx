## Overview

This DataOps initiative aimed to modernize an analytics stack that was split across legacy warehouse systems and ad hoc pipelines. The solution introduced a scalable lakehouse model that improved consumption across engineering, analytics, and product teams.

---

## Key Highlights

- Designed ingestion pipelines for batch, event, and near-real-time data sources.
- Standardized data contracts and schema versioning to improve downstream trust and compatibility.
- Implemented orchestration with Airflow and dbt to manage data quality and transformation consistency.
- Enabled self-serve analytics through curated datasets, quality checks, and shared semantic models.
- Created monitoring for pipeline health, freshness, and lineage across the data estate.


---

## Outcome

The organization gained a more reliable, scalable analytics layer with better time-to-insight and less manual troubleshooting.
